What we do

Swap it. Boost it.
Tune it. Build it.

GM and Ford specialists. Engine swaps, forced induction, dyno tuning and fabrication all under one roof, so the car leaves finished and dialed in.

Engine swaps

LS, LT and Coyote swaps into classics, muscle and modern chassis. Mounts, harness, ECU, fuel, cooling and exhaust handled start to finish.

  • LS / LT swaps
  • Coyote swaps
  • Harness & ECU integration
  • Fuel & cooling systems

Forced induction

Single and twin turbo systems and supercharger installs, built around your power goal and fuel. Fabricated hot side, piping and intercooler, then tuned on the dyno.

  • Turbo & twin-turbo kits
  • Supercharger installs
  • Custom manifolds & piping
  • Fuel system upgrades

Dyno tuning

Custom calibration on our in-house dyno. Factory ECUs are calibrated with HP Tuners. We also offer Holley EFI and HP Tuners CORE standalone ECU systems for swaps and race builds.

  • Factory ECU tuning via HP Tuners
  • Holley EFI systems
  • HP Tuners CORE ECU
  • Street, track & race tunes

Custom fabrication

One-off parts built in-house. Turbo manifolds, intercooler piping, engine and trans mounts, brackets, exhaust and roll cages.

  • TIG & MIG welding
  • Exhaust & headers
  • Roll cages & chassis
  • Mounts & brackets

Diagnostics

Drivability problems, fueling and ignition faults, sensor and wiring issues on modified cars. Data-logged and traced to the root cause, not parts-cannon guessing.

  • Datalog analysis
  • Fuel & ignition diagnosis
  • Wiring & sensor faults
  • Pre-tune inspections

Full builds

Show-and-go cars from the ground up: race cars that run the number and show cars that take the trophy. Planned, built, tuned and finished here.

  • Drag & race builds
  • Show car builds
  • Restomod & pro-touring
  • Complete drivetrain packages

About the shop

From reliable street-driven builds to full race cars, tuning, installs and fabrication all happen under one roof, so the car gets finished the way it was drawn up.
